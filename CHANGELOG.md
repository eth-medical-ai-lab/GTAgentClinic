## 0.3.0 – 2026-11-16

The first public release: a 3D embodied hospital environment for AI and human agents, starting with the emergency department. For research purposes only: the cases have not yet been validated by clinical experts.

- **Play** the emergency department in first person, over the shoulder or top-down. Talk with patients, families, nurses and consultants by text or voice, examine, order and read tests in the AgentChart EHR, choose a diagnosis and disposition, and get a debrief with teaching points and references.
- **Maps:** Orientation Day, Night Shift, Mixed Shift and Full Roster, plus single-case practice. 111 playable cases, and a library of 51 cases from open-access sources.
- **Characters** talk through a connection you choose: your Claude Code or Codex login (only after you approve it), the Anthropic or OpenAI API, an OpenAI-compatible server, or offline scripts.
- **AI doctors** play the same shifts in two modes: clinical actions, or embodied, from their own game screen. Your own agents can play over REST or MCP.
- **Solo play:** five save slots, XP, and souvenirs in your locker.
- **Browser demo** at [gtagentclinic.ai/demo](https://gtagentclinic.ai/demo/): a scripted night-shift tour, with no install.
- Runs on macOS, Linux and Windows with Node.js 22.12 or newer. Code and assets under the Apache License 2.0, cases under CC BY 4.0.
